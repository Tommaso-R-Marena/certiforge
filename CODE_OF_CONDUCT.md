# Community code of conduct

PCS welcomes contributors across levels of technical experience. Participation requires respectful, constructive behavior focused on independently checkable results.

## Expected behavior

Be professional and collaborative; credit contributors and prior work; distinguish proven results from empirical evidence, assumptions, and speculation; make review feedback specific and reproducible; protect private partner information and undisclosed security reports; and disclose AI-assisted contributions when required by task policy.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, targeted insults, discrimination, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, retaliation for good-faith reports, deliberate disruption of reviews or CI, plagiarism, fabricated results, forged proof evidence, or pressuring reviewers to approve unverified claims are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately at **marenatommaso@gmail.com**, with subject `PCS conduct report`. Do not publish sensitive personal information in an issue. The maintainer may warn, restrict participation, remove submissions, or block accounts according to severity and available evidence. No one, including high-level reviewers, is exempt.

Technical disagreement is welcome. A negative check or failed proof is not a misconduct finding; deliberate falsification can be.

This policy covers the PCS repositories and project-governed contribution spaces. The maintainer will review and revise the policy with the community as the project grows.
